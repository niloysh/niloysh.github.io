About Me

I’m a PhD candidate in the Systems and Networking Group at the University of Waterloo, working with Prof. Raouf Boutaba. My research spans several areas in systems and networking, including 5G networks, AI-driven network management and orchestration, and software-defined networking.

Before joining UWaterloo, I worked in the Smart Wireless Applications and Networking Group at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur with Prof. Sudip Misra.

For more information about my research, check out some of my current and past projects and publications.

---
title: "Preprint: NWDAF-Enabled Analytics in 5G Networks"
date: 2025-05-11T09:00:00-05:00
---
Managing 5G networks at scale demands automation that can adapt in real time, yet existing tools struggle to combine standardized data collection, machine learning integration, and closed-loop control.  

Our ongoing work addresses this gap by exploring how the 3GPP Network Data Analytics Function (NWDAF) can be realized in practice to support low-latency, scalable, and intelligent network management. The approach shows promise for use cases such as anomaly detection, security, and policy enforcement.  

This work is now available as a [preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.06789).

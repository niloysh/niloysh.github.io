Our paper "Q-Soft: QoS-aware Traffic Forwarding in Software-Defined Cyber-Physical Systems" is accepted in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal. This work was done at the [Smart Wireless Applications and Networking (SWAN)](http://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/~smisra/swan/) Lab at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, India, under the guidance of Prof. [Sudip Misra](https://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/~smisra/).

---
title: "SliceScope: SLA-Aware Monitoring in 5G Slices"
date: 2025-09-13T09:00:00-05:00
---
**SliceScope** is an ongoing research effort on SLA-aware monitoring for 5G network slices.  
Guaranteeing SLAs demands visibility that can adapt to rapidly changing conditions, yet current approaches are either too coarse or too costly.  
SliceScope investigates how to provide accurate, adaptive monitoring that enables early detection of violations without incurring excessive overhead.

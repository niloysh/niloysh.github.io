The AmbuSens system was developed at the [Smart Wireless Applications and Networking (SWAN)](http://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/~smisra/swan/) Lab at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, India.

AmbuSens focuses on achieving seamless real-time monitoring and management of healthcare data in an ambulatory context, leveraging IoT technologies such as wireless on-body sensors, cellular (3G/4G), and cloud computing. The system leverages wireless body area network (WBAN) to wirelessly collect various physiological health parameters (e.g., ECG and heart rate). The collected data is processed by a middleware solution that uses an unique hashing-based privacy-preservation mechanism to preserve patient's confidentiality before subsequent transmission and storage in the cloud.

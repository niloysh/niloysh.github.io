I'm a PhD student in the [Systems and Networking Group](https://syn.uwaterloo.ca/) at the University of Waterloo, working with Prof. [Raouf Boutaba](http://rboutaba.cs.uwaterloo.ca/). My research spans several areas in systems and networking, including 5G networks, AI-driven network management and orchestration, and software-defined networking.

Before joining UWaterloo, I worked in the [Smart Wireless Applications and Networking](http://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/~smisra/swan/) Group at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur with Prof. [Sudip Misra](https://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/~smisra/).

For more information about my research, check out some of my current and past [projects](#Projects) and [publications](/publications).
